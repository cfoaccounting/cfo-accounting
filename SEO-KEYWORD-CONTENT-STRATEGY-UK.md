# CFO Accounting — UK SEO, Content & Search Intent Strategy

Updated: 20 August 2026

## Positioning used across the revised website

The site now positions CFO Accounting as a practical outsourced finance team for UK small businesses: everyday accounting and compliance when owners need things handled, plus management reporting, funding support and Virtual CFO guidance when they need better decisions.

The writing deliberately leads with outcomes — time saved, fewer deadline surprises, clearer cash and profit visibility, less admin, stronger funding readiness and better decisions — before explaining the technical service.

## Local UK keyword pattern

Google local visibility is influenced by relevance, distance and prominence. If CFO Accounting has a genuine staffed office or eligible Google Business Profile location, use location modifiers such as:

- small business accountant [city]
- accountant near me
- bookkeeper [city]
- VAT accountant [city]
- payroll services [city]
- limited company accountant [city]
- virtual CFO [city]

Do not create fake city pages or invent offices. If the business is UK-wide and remote only, keep the core site focused on UK-wide and online-accountant intent, then build location pages only for real service areas supported by genuine business information.

## E-E-A-T and trust work implemented / recommended

Implemented in this revision:

- Named team and experience remain visible on About and Team pages.
- Financial copy avoids unverified awards, review scores, regulator badges and ranking claims.
- The grants guide now has a visible review/update line and an author link to the Team page.
- Page titles and descriptions are unique and service-specific.
- FAQ and breadcrumb structured data remain in place, with breadcrumb URLs corrected to the live clean URL structure.
- UK service terminology is aligned with common HMRC / GOV.UK wording such as Self Assessment, Company Tax Return, VAT, PAYE, CIS, company accounts and Corporation Tax.
- Internal links connect core services, related services, funding resources and conversion pages.

Recommended after launch:

- Add the exact registered entity, Companies House number, AML supervisor/reference and professional memberships only after they are verified.
- Add a real office address only if the business genuinely operates from and can represent that location.
- Build and fully complete a Google Business Profile if eligible.
- Collect genuine client reviews and respond to them consistently.
- Add named author/reviewer blocks to all future tax and funding articles.
- Cite GOV.UK/HMRC and other primary sources in financial guidance articles, especially where rules or deadlines can change.
- Add real case studies with the client’s permission and anonymise commercially sensitive figures where needed.
- Review Search Console queries after 6–12 weeks and expand pages around terms already generating impressions.

## Technical changes made for Vercel image reliability

- All local image paths now start from the site root, for example `/assets/images/bookkeeping-accounting-support.webp` rather than `assets/images/...`.
- Logos, favicon, shared CSS and shared JavaScript also use root-absolute paths.
- This prevents image URLs from becoming dependent on the current clean URL or trailing-slash behaviour.
- The requested homepage team image has been downloaded into the project and is served locally as `/assets/images/home-hero-accounting-consultation.webp`, reducing reliance on a third-party image request.
- Local asset verification passes with zero missing files.
